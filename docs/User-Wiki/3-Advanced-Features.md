# Advanced Features

Ready to become a BookBoss power user? Here are some of the advanced tools available to you.

## 1. Bulk Operations
Need to delete or edit a bunch of books at once? 
1. While looking at your library grid, click the **"Select"** or **"Bulk Edit"** icon (usually near the search bar).
2. Checkboxes will appear on all your books.
3. Select the ones you want to change.
4. Use the action menu that pops up to apply a single change (like changing shelves or deleting) to all selected books simultaneously.

## 2. Advanced Search
The main search bar at the top isn't just for exact titles. 
- You can type an author's name, part of a title, or even scan a barcode/ISBN directly into it to instantly filter your view.

## 3. Reading Lists
Wait, how are Reading Lists different from Shelves?
- **Shelves** are broad categories for organizing your whole physical/digital library (like genres).
- **Reading Lists** are specific, ordered queues of books you plan to read in a specific order.

## 4. Book Borrowing / Loans
Did you lend a physical book to a friend? 
- Open the book's details.
- Go to the **Loan** tab.
- Click **"Mark as Loaned Out"**.
- Enter your friend's name and when it's due back. BookBoss will remind you if they keep it too long!
