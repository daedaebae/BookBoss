# Basic Features

Once you've settled into the BookBoss interface, it's time to start adding and organizing your books!

## 1. Adding a Book
Adding a book to your library is easy.
1. Click the **"Add Book"** button (usually at the top of your library view).
2. A modal window will pop up.
3. **The Magic Way**: Enter the book's ISBN number and click the search icon. BookBoss will fetch the title, author, and cover automatically!
4. **The Manual Way**: Fill out the title, author, format, and other details manually.
5. Click **Save**.

## 2. Using Shelves
Shelves are like folders for your books (e.g., "Favorites", "Summer Reading", "To Read").

1. Look at your Sidebar on the left. Find the **Shelves** section.
2. Click the **+** (plus) icon next to the "Shelves" title.
3. Type a name for your new shelf and hit Enter.
4. To add a book to a shelf, click on a book in your library to open its details, then select the shelf from its dropdown menu!

*(Imagine a screenshot of a created Shelf in the UI here)*

## 3. Tracking Your Reading Status
You can track if you've finished a book or are currently reading it.
- Open any book to view its details.
- Use the **Status** dropdown to change it to "In Progress", "Completed", or "DNF" (Did Not Finish).
- You can now quickly pull up these lists using the filters on the left Sidebar!
